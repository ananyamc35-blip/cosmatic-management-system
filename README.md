Introduction
A Cosmetics Management System is a web-based application designed to manage cosmetic products efficiently. It helps cosmetic stores, beauty shops, and wholesalers maintain product information, inventory, sales records, customer details, and supplier data. The system reduces manual work, improves accuracy, and enhances business productivity.
Objectives
To maintain cosmetic product records digitally.
To manage stock availability and inventory.
To track sales and purchases.
To maintain customer and supplier information.
To generate reports for business analysis.
To reduce paperwork and human errors.
